The challenge

Industrial companies need more than a simple resale marketplace. They need verified exchanges, shared logistics, legal traceability and clear environmental reporting to turn unused resources into measurable value.

What I delivered

  • Private-cloud foundation OpenStack virtual machines, secure networking and Kubernetes nodes for the application stack.
  • Automated delivery GitLab CI/CD pipelines connecting code changes to tested frontend and backend deployments.
  • Observable operations Prometheus metrics, Grafana dashboards and infrastructure visibility for Kubernetes workloads.
  • Business workflows Role-based Angular spaces for companies, transporters and administrators.

Product scope

The platform centralizes listings, stocks, reservations, deliveries, transactions, events and sustainability analytics. AI-assisted modules support buyer-seller matching, material classification, damaged-product detection and operational anomaly detection.
